One Record Journal – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026

One Record Journal (“the app”) is a personal mood journal. It is built so that your journal belongs to you: the developer does not run any servers, does not collect analytics, and cannot see your entries.

Information the app stores

The app stores the content you create, such as your daily mood, notes, photos, an attached song, and weather. All of it is saved on your device and in your own iCloud account. Health readings are the exception: they stay on your device (see Apple Health below). Nothing is sent to the developer.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, your entries are synced between your devices through Apple’s CloudKit, using your private iCloud database. Only you can access this data. Apple’s handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Apple Health (HealthKit)

  • Read: with your permission, the app reads your sleep, step count, and activity time so it can show them as stickers on your entries.
  • Write: if you turn on the option in Settings, the app saves the mood you pick to Apple Health as a Daily Mood (State of Mind).

Your health readings are kept only on your device, and are not backed up or synced through iCloud. An entry syncs only which health stickers it shows, and each of your devices reads the values from its own Apple Health.

Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, is never sold, and is never shared with third parties. It is never stored on any server operated by the developer. You can change or revoke access at any time in the Health app or in Settings › Privacy & Security › Health.

Location and weather

With your permission, the app uses your approximate location (about 1 km accuracy) to look up the current weather through Apple WeatherKit. The app saves only the weather conditions and a place name with your entry. Your coordinates are not stored.

Photos and camera

If you choose to attach a photo, the app uses only the photo you pick or take. It does not scan or upload your photo library.

App lock (Face ID, Touch ID, or passcode)

If you turn on the app lock in Settings, the app asks iOS to confirm it’s you with Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode. iOS does the check and tells the app only whether it succeeded. The app never receives, stores, or sends your face, fingerprint, or passcode.

Sharing and exporting

When you choose to share an entry or a month summary, the app creates an image of it on your device and hands it to the iOS share sheet. When you export your journal, the app creates a spreadsheet file (CSV) on your device with your entries, including the health readings this device has. Either way, it goes only where you send it. Nothing is shared unless you pick a destination.

Daily reminder

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules the notifications on your device. No server is involved, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings.

Apple Music

If you attach a song, the app searches the Apple Music catalog through Apple’s MusicKit and saves the song’s title, artist, and artwork with your entry.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual entries in the app. To delete everything, delete the app and remove its data from iCloud in Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
